# TEAMORA AI — Customer Onboarding & Quick Start Guide

1. **Create or Switch Organization:** Use the header workspace selector or launch the Setup Wizard from **Help & Support**.
2. **Configure Language & Currency:** Toggle `EN` (LTR) or `اردو` (RTL) and select `USD ($)` or `PKR (₨)`.
3. **Invite Team Members:** Open **Team & Permissions** to invite colleagues with least-privilege RBAC roles (`admin`, `manager`, `member`, `viewer`).
4. **Seed Knowledge & Dispatch Tasks:** Add policies to Knowledge Hub and assign tasks to your 15 AI Employees. High-risk operations always require explicit human sign-off in **Approvals Gate**.
5. **Export Data Anytime:** Managers, Admins, and Owners can export JSON or CSV archives from **Security & Audit Logs** regardless of plan billing status.
